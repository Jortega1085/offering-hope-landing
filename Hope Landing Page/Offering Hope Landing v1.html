The Keynote

Built To Break

What your body is carrying that your mind won't say.

Chronic tension. Insomnia. Rage. Burnout. Back, jaw, gut pain that no scan can explain. The body keeps the unprocessed — and eventually, it speaks for what the mind refused to.

This keynote names what's underneath, in front of a room, with a live somatic exercise that gets every hand in the air inside the first sixty seconds.

Meet Hope

The work, the woman,
the lineage.

Hope is Lakota — Rosebud — and was raised in Comanche ways. She carries two nations in her body and two traditions of understanding the world. Every keynote opens with a formal land acknowledgment before her personal story is told.

For sixteen years she has sat with people in the place between the wound and the work. She is a somatic practitioner and an intuitive life coach. The clinical training came first. The ceremonial knowing was always there.
