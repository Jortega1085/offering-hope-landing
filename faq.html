Common questions

What is nervous system coaching?

Nervous system coaching helps you read and regulate what your body is carrying — the tension, the 3 a.m. wake-ups, the short fuse — instead of pushing through it. Hope Kimple teaches it through three pillars: somatic (body), mental (mind), and intuitive (gut) awareness.

Who is Hope Kimple?

Hope Kimple is a Nervous System Coach and Intuitive Life Coach based in Hayward, California, with sixteen-plus years of clinical experience working with chronic pain. She works with people through workshops, one-on-one coaching, and keynote speaking.

When and where is the next event?

The next workshop is The Rebuild, a two-hour session in the East Bay. The date isn't set yet — get on the list and you'll hear as soon as it's scheduled. The Built To Break keynote runs periodically and is always free to attend.

How much do the workshops cost?

The Built To Break keynote is free to attend. The Rebuild, the paid next step after the keynote, is two hours for $30. That's the only workshop price.

What is the 90-day coaching container?

It's one-on-one coaching over ninety days through all three pillars — body, mind, and gut — applied to your life. The founding rate is $1,200 for the first five clients before August 8 (official rate $2,497).

Does Hope speak at corporate events?

Yes. Hope delivers the Built To Break keynote (60–90 minutes, with workshop and half-day formats) for corporate teams, first responders, and conferences. Request the speaker one-sheet at hope@offeringhope.co.

Aho is a handcrafted topical made by Hope. It contains cannabis, so it's sold only through licensed California dispensaries — not online. See the Aho page for current locations.
