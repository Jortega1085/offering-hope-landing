Long Live Hope

Keep walking.
You were never meant to do it alone.

The change doesn't happen in two hours. It happens in the weeks after. When the old patterns come back knocking and you need somewhere to bring them.

That's what this is. A room you belong to. A rhythm you can count on. People walking the same path, every single month.

Long Live Hope is a monthly membership built on three of my frameworks, turning in an open cycle. Fear vs. Intuition. Break the Spell. Boundaries & Becoming. There's no fixed start, and no catching up. Whenever you join is exactly where you're meant to be.

How a month works

Here's how a month works. We take one theme, and we go through it twice.

First, we do the work. One live group call where I teach the theme and we practice it together, so you leave with something real to live in.

Then we come back and reflect. A second call about two weeks later. What came up, what's shifting, where you got stuck. We work it through while it's still warm, instead of leaving you alone with it for a month.

This is group coaching, so you're never on the spot. Bring something to work on if you want, or just show up and take it in. Sharing is always yours to choose.

What you get

  • Two live group calls every month. One to do the work, one to reflect on it.
  • Three frameworks in open rotation. Always present and forward. We don't dig up the past.
  • A group walking the same path, online-first, so you can join from anywhere.
  • Bring what you want, or just take it in. Sharing is always optional.
  • The occasional in-person gathering, as a treat.
  • Come as you are. Every single month.
